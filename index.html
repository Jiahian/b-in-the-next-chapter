What counts as a point?

Any activity completed out of personal initiative can be clocked!

  1. Distance accumulated in a physical/sports activity
  2. Time spent with a friend/CG mates
  3. Time spent reaching out/meeting another's needs
  4. Personal time spent praying for something
  5. Joining prayer meetings
The list is non-exhaustive. There are no hard and fast rules! As long as an effort/initiative is made, feel free to clock it! ☺️
